Flex is the platform behind the GOV.UK app. It runs serverless on AWS, and lets the app personalise
what it shows by bringing together:

- the GOV.UK app data and settings for a user such as their linked accounts and notification preferences
- the government services the GOV.UK app is built to work with

This means each of those services can respond to a known person instead of an anonymous request.

Teams outside the platform write **domains**. Domains hold the logic behind personalised
features. Flex provides everything around the domains:

- a **Frontdoor** that checks, authenticates and routes every request
- a home for each **domain**, with its own API and its own path from development to production
- **gateways**, which are a domain's access to other services. They validate everything that passes
  through them.

See [The platform](/flex-platform/start/platform/) for how the three parts fit together and what
exists today.
